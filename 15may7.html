Arrangement of cows in stalls


There are 11 empty stalls in a row.
You must arrange 10 cows and leave 1 stall empty.

There are three breeds of cows:
Breed A: 3 cows, each has a 60% chance of being aggressive.
Breed B: 5 cows, each has a 30% chance of being aggressive.
Breed C: 2 cows, each has a 10% chance of being aggressive.

Assume each cow's aggression is independent.

Your goal is to arrange the cows so that the probability of two aggressive cows being in adjacent occupied stalls is as low as possible.

Question:
Give one arrangement of the cows and the empty stall that minimizes this probability.
