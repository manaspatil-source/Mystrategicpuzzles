Climbing a Staircase


Suppose you have to climb a staircase with 20 steps

You can climb either one step, or move on the step after the next, paying the costs for each

The cost of climbing any step is determined as such:

1)For steps that are prime numbers, the cost is $1

2) For steps that are even numbers(including 2) the cost is $4

3) For steps that are multiples of 3(including 3 itself) the cost is $9

4) For steps that are multiples of 5, including 5 itself the cost is $25

If a step fulfills multiple criteria simultaneously, its cost is the product of the costs(for example, 6 is a multiple of both 2 and 3, so its cost is 4*9=36)

What is the sequence of steps you move in to get to the top in minimum cost/ (Note to get to the top your final step must be in position 19 or 20, and you can assume position 1 to be a prime number)
