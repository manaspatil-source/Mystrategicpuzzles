Heads and Tails

You and your friend each choose either Heads or Tails.

Your payoff is:

Heads / Heads = ₹20
Heads / Tails = -₹10
Tails / Heads = -₹12
Tails / Tails = ₹30

Suppose you believe your friend will choose Heads with probability p%.

Question:
At what value of p are you indifferent between choosing Heads and Tails?
