Muddy Head


You and three friends — M, N, and O — are standing together. Some of you may have mud on your heads.

Each person can see everyone else's head, but cannot see their own.

You can see that M and O have muddy heads.

Person A asks everyone publicly:
“Does at least one person here have a muddy head?”

You answer: “Yes.”

Then Person A repeatedly asks everyone publicly:
“Do you know whether your own head is muddy?”

Everyone must answer honestly with one of three options:
“Yes, I have mud.”
“No, I do not have mud.”
“I don’t know.”

In the first round, nobody says they know.
In the second round, nobody says they know.
In the third round, Person A asks again.

What should you say?
